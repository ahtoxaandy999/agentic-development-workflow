## Gate question and assessment

Can the reference's claimed reduction in repeated prompt content, missing
identity/freshness/result/payload/custody fields and correction loops be tested
without making the reference operative, inventing contextual values, selecting
new tooling or relaxing accepted boundaries?

**YES, provided every use is separately selected and authorized, the first
result is assessed before a second task is selected, and all missing contextual
inputs fail closed.**

Assessment:

- **Accepted reference — PASS.** Exact candidate, review and coordinator
  disposition are durable and current.
- **Distinct consumer need — PASS.** Future evidence producers, independent
  reviewers and coordinators are identifiable consumers.
- **Falsifiable hypothesis — PASS.** The accepted reference names observable
  benefit and abandonment measures.
- **Representative diversity — PASS WITH CONDITION.** A documentation-only
  task and a materially different code/test or multi-file task can exercise
  different evidence burdens, but neither actual task exists or is selected
  here.
- **State ownership — PASS.** Measurements can be recorded as immutable
  task-local evidence while the Register retains the current gate.
- **Mechanism-selection boundary — PASS.** No new App, MCP, skill, hook,
  Action, schema, validator, generator, database or orchestration is required.
- **Authority boundary — PASS.** A selection gate can bind an actual future
  task without authorizing routine or automated use.
- **Operational prerequisites — unresolved and fail closed.** Actors,
  authority, exact subject, custody, retention, permissions and effect limits
  must be supplied by the later task-specific grant.

## Evaluation shape

The bounded evaluation has at most two sequential slots:

1. **RU-1: documentation-oriented task.** Select one future, not-yet-started,
   bounded ADW task whose primary work product is a Markdown design, review,
   disposition or persistence artifact and whose normal lifecycle already
   requires producer verification.
2. **RU-2: materially different task.** Consider one future code-and-test,
   multi-file, or otherwise materially different task only after RU-1 has
   completed and the coordinator has assessed its evidence and burden.

Each slot requires its own coordinator selection and execution authority. RU-2
is not reserved, selected or authorized by this record. The evaluation ends
after two completed qualifying tasks or earlier under the abandonment rule.

The same task must not be counted twice merely because it has W, C and I
subjects or multiple verification obligations. A task counts only after its
bounded evidence use and applicable review/assessment have completed.

## Selection contract for RU-1

A later selection gate may choose RU-1 only when it can bind:

- a concrete task ID, objective and authoritative task contract;
- the repository and exact current base where relevant;
- the subject class to be verified and the exact W, C or I identity available
  at each reliance point;
- one attributable producer/verifier and the exact bounded verification grant;
- one supervisor, evidence custodian and escalation owner;
- complete required criteria and accountable N/A decisions;
- permitted read and write effects, including exact evidence output paths;
- secret-safe payload and redaction boundaries;
- contextual retention/retrieval requirements;
- the independent reviewer or later review-selection rule;
- stop, interruption, recovery and stale-subject behavior;
- the metrics below and the location of immutable results.

If these values are not supplied from their authorized owners, selection fails
closed. The gate must not invent a person, duration, permission, criterion,
subject identity or evidence destination.

## Required observations

Each completed representative task must record, without fabricated precision:

- how many required fields still needed clarification after the reference was
  supplied;
- which identity, freshness, result, payload or custody omissions were detected
  before independent review;
- review findings attributable to the evidence representation;
- approximate duplicated prompt or reference content avoided, stated as a
  transparent estimate rather than a token-savings guarantee;
- ambiguity, maintenance effort or additional ceremony introduced by use of
  the reference;
- fields or sections that were unused, confusing or insufficient;
- whether the task would have been materially less reliable without the
  reference;
- the coordinator conclusion: useful, inconclusive or not useful for that
  task class.

Passing task checks alone do not prove utility. A finding-free review alone does
not prove utility. The conclusion must consider both omissions prevented and
burden introduced.

## Use boundary

For a selected task, the reference may be used only as explanatory guidance to
form one task-local producer-verification evidence package. The actual task
contract and authoritative owners determine every contextual value.

The reference must not be treated as:

- an operative schema or validation grammar;
- a mandatory repository-wide template;
- a generator, parser or executable checklist;
- permission to collect secrets or unsafe payloads;
- proof of provenance, authenticity, custody, reviewer identity or retention;
- permission to write a repository, invoke tooling or advance a task state;
- a replacement for independent review, coordinator disposition or acceptance.

Evidence production and repository persistence remain separate grants. The
producer cannot accept its own work. The reviewer must bind the exact immutable
subject and independently retrieve controlling sources.

## State ownership and durable evidence

The selected task's existing authoritative task record owns its current
verification projection. A representative-use result is immutable evidence
about that one task and must point to the authoritative task state; it must not
become a competing tracker.

The Research Register owns the ADW current gate and durable pointers. This
scoping record owns only the evaluation contract. Later selection records own
slot assignment. Task-local evidence packages own publication-time
observations. A later coordinator assessment owns the utility disposition.

Chat, memory, local drafts and runner status are not mutable authoritative
owners.

## Abandonment and continuation rules

Stop after RU-1 without selecting RU-2 when any of the following is true:

- the reference introduces a material semantic conflict or duplicate owner;
- required contextual values cannot be supplied safely;
- use requires an unselected mechanism or an unauthorized operational effect;
- representation overhead materially exceeds omissions or ambiguity avoided;
- the task cannot preserve exact identity, freshness, custody or review
  binding;
- a BLOCKER or MAJOR finding shows the reference itself is misleading;
- an RG or DI boundary would need to be weakened.

A correctable task-specific defect does not automatically reject the reference.
Preserve the episode, correct through a new identity where required, and assess
whether the reference caused, prevented or merely exposed the defect.

After RU-1, the coordinator must decide one of:

- authorize selection of one materially different RU-2 task;
- revise or re-review the reference through a new candidate lifecycle;
- stop the evaluation as not useful or too costly;
- defer for lack of a qualifying task.

No choice is automatic.
