supplied:

2. contextual retention duration and custody boundary;
3. the manifest self-digest limit;
4. the meaning of repository non-effect where no checkout exists.

None required new product intent, mechanism selection or broader authority.
They were legitimate contextual inputs rather than omissions in the accepted
reference.

### Omissions caught before review

The producer attributed nine categories of caught omissions to applying the
reference:

- exact live tree and controlling blob inventory;
- output-root and destination-absence preflight;
- explicit W classification with C and I absent;
- item-specific freshness limits;
- complete owner mapping;
- payload paths, media, encoding, bytes, digests and redaction treatment;
- governed N/A accounting without pass credit;
- per-walkthrough non-authority statements;
- explicit non-implications for review, disposition, acceptance, authority,
  RG resolution and Workflow v1 adoption.

These are material to identity, freshness, custody and authority separation.
They are not cosmetic formatting gains.

### Review outcome

The independent candidate review found no candidate defect and confirmed the
same architecture could be derived through all required sections,
walkthroughs and adversarial scenarios. No review finding was attributed to
the evidence representation.

The review did not independently audit every local evidence-package byte and
therefore does not prove the package generally correct. Its zero-finding
candidate result is corroborating outcome evidence, not sole proof of utility
or causality.

### Duplication avoided

The producer estimated 4-6 KiB of repeated explanatory prompt/reference prose
was avoided by pointing to stable subject, obligation, payload, freshness,
custody and non-implication meanings. This is a transparent rough estimate,
not a measured token, cost, latency or productivity saving.

### Ceremony and maintenance burden

The reference introduced moderate ceremony:

- five retained files for one Markdown work product;
- 27257 bytes of evidence and utility material around a 31350-byte subject;
- cross-file digest and custody bookkeeping;
- a self-referential manifest-identity boundary requiring external binding;
- several governed N/A decisions for W-only work;
- manual semantic reading in addition to deterministic presence checks.

This burden is material. It prevents a conclusion that the current form should
be mandatory or routinely applied to every small documentation task.

### Unused and unexercised scope

Candidate C, integration I, publication readback, redaction derivatives,
recovery, interruption and custody-failure branches were not exercised by the
local W-only production episode. The later candidate lifecycle established C
and independent review separately, but it did not turn the original local
package into publication-time C/I evidence.

No accepted reference section proved insufficient. The only explicit local
interpretation concerned manifest self-identity.

RU-1 meets the scoping contract's `useful` outcome for a documentation-oriented
task because:

- omissions prevented were numerous and materially related to accepted
  authority and evidence boundaries;
- the resulting candidate was byte-stable through persistence and accepted
  after a fresh zero-finding independent review;
- no semantic conflict, duplicate current-state owner, optional mechanism or
  unauthorized effect was introduced;
- required contextual values were supplied without inventing product intent;
- the evidence remained inspectable and retrievable through final assessment.

Confidence is moderate, not high. The result is one case, producer-attributed
benefit is not a controlled comparison, and evidence volume was nearly as
large as the work product. RU-1 does not support a general claim that the
reference saves time or cost.

The correct continuation is one RU-2 selection gate, not routine adoption.
RU-2 should use a naturally arising, future, not-yet-started code-and-test or
multi-file task whose evidence profile is materially different. It must test
whether identity and custody benefits persist while ceremony remains
proportionate. A task created solely to generate favorable evidence is not a
qualifying representative use.
