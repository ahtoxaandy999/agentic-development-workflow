## Required content contract

The reference must cover, at minimum:

1. authority and non-authority, including that the artifact owns no mutable
   task, verification, custody, repository or workflow state;
2. an annotated, generic producer-verification manifest representation;
3. manifest/evidence identity and exact task, contract, authorization, run,
   operation and attempt identifiers where applicable;
4. exact verification-subject classification and binding for mutable working
   output `W`, immutable candidate `C`, and integrated subject `I`;
5. bounded verification scope, governing criteria, enumerated required
   obligations and separately justified legitimate N/A decisions;
6. expected and observed preconditions, including exact base, checkout, input,
   dependency and relevant configuration identities;
7. actual verification method, command, request or inspection description,
   while keeping prose non-executable;
8. attributable producer/verifier, role, authority reference, observation time
   and material clock limitations;
9. load-bearing tool, runtime, version, configuration, model and input
   identities only where material;
10. per-obligation results, conclusion, failures, skips, unavailable evidence,
    limitations and affected impact/carry-forward references;
11. item-specific freshness and exact correct-subject evidence binding at the
    point of reliance;
12. payload inventory with safe relative paths, media or encoding, byte count
    and SHA-256 for every retained payload;
13. secret/redaction treatment and explicit failure when required evidence
    cannot be retained or retrieved safely;
14. publication-time custodian, authorized readers, contextual retention
    obligation and required retrieval points without inventing actual values;
15. immutable package identity after future persistence as repository, full
    containing commit, path, Git blob and optional fragment; and
16. explicit identity limits: equality does not prove truth, correctness,
    completeness, authority, authenticity, independence, accessibility,
    retention or acceptance.

The representation may use YAML-like annotated syntax for readability, but it
must say explicitly that labels and layout are illustrative, not normative
serialization, schema, API, validator or live checklist.

## Required fictional examples

Use compact, obviously fictional and non-secret examples demonstrating:

1. complete current producer verification for `W`;
2. verification bound to immutable `C`;
3. a required obligation that failed or was skipped;
4. stale or wrong-subject evidence;
5. legitimate governed N/A with no satisfaction value; and
6. unsafe, unavailable or inaccessible required payload that blocks reliance
   pending a separate custody decision.

Example digests and identities must be unmistakably placeholders and must not
be presented as valid repository objects or real actors. Examples must not
silently turn absence, N/A, skip, failure, stale evidence, an aggregate green
status or a digest alone into pass.

## Utility and abandonment boundary

The materialization carries the scoping record's falsifiable hypothesis: it
should reduce repeated prompt restatement, missing identity/freshness/result/
payload/custody fields and evidence-representation correction loops.

Before any schema, generator or broader evidence-reference family is
considered, at least two separately authorized representative tasks must
record clarification needs, representation omissions, related review
findings, approximate duplicated prompt/reference content avoided, and any new
ambiguity or maintenance burden.

If no material benefit appears, stop expansion. Existence or acceptance of the
reference cannot justify schema, validator or automation promotion by itself.
